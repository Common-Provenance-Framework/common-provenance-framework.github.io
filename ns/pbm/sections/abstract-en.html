Abstract

The Provenance of Biological Material Ontology (PBM-O) expresses the Provenance of Biological Material (PBM) structures using the OWL 2 Web Ontology Language. The PBM structures are specializations of the provenance structures defined in W3C PROV-DM and are used together with the Common Provenance Model (CPM) to represent the provenance of biological material, such as samples and the activities performed on them. PBM-O is an extension of PROV-O.
